A chaired panel session, including an introductory presentation, examining the opportunities and challenges of AI foundation models for Earth observation. The discussion considered scientific applications, trust and transparency, European capability, compute and data infrastructure, skills and partnerships, and the roadmap needed for foundation models to deliver lasting scientific and societal value.
